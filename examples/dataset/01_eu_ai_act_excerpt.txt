Article 9. Providers of high-risk AI systems must establish and document a risk
management system, and must perform a risk assessment before the system is placed
on the market.
Article 13. High-risk AI systems must be designed so that their operation is
sufficiently transparent, and the provider shall disclose to users that they are
interacting with an AI system.
Article 14. High-risk AI systems must allow effective human oversight, including
the ability for a person to override an automated decision.
