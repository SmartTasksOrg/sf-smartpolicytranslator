Article 1. Providers of high-risk AI systems must perform a documented risk
assessment before the system is placed on the market.
Article 2. Personal data used to operate the system shall be redacted of
directly identifying information, and secrets must never be sent to a
third-party model.
Article 3. Where an automated decision produces a legal effect on a person, the
provider must offer human review of that decision.
Article 4. The provider shall disclose to affected users that they are
interacting with an AI system.
Article 5. Model outputs used in a compliance decision must be verified for
accuracy, and a receipt of that verification should be retained in an audit log.
Article 6. Prompts sent to a model must be inspected for injection attempts.
Article 7. Access to models must be routed through an authorization gateway.
